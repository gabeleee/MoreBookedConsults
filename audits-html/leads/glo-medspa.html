Consult booking

More booked consults from the traffic you already have

Today every Book Now button goes to the Contact page, a general form that asks for a country code and picks from 10 service categories. On a phone, the first screen shows two call buttons and a cookie banner covering the lower half, but no way to book. Four changes raise the share of visitors who book, starting in month 1.

1Start the form in the homepage heroThe first screen asks one easy question, so booking starts where visitors already are.
2Ask about them, not the menuClients pick a goal like lines or volume, and your providers choose the treatment.

4One form everywhereThe same short form on every treatment page, the Specials page and every blog post, for both locations.

Content

Become the go-to med spa for every treatment you offer

Google favors the site that covers a topic most completely and links it together well (Koray Tuğberk GÜBÜR's topical authority method). Glo already has pages and posts for Botox, fillers, HydraFacial and IPL, but they work alone instead of as one map. HydraFacial shows it: three pages cover it, and Google ranks your Face page instead.

Every page feeds the pages that bookYour blog posts on Restylane Kysse, PDO threads and IPL can send readers to the right treatment page and the consult form.
New pages rank fasterOnce a topic is fully covered, Google tends to trust and rank new pages on it sooner.
One page per searchGoogle's top result for Botox in Wilmington is your Specials page, which has 5 words of text.
Both locations on the mapYour Google markup has no address for Wilmington or Winterville, and its phone number is one digit off.
