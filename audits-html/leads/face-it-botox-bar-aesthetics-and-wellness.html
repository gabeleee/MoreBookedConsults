Become the go-to med spa for every treatment you offer

Consult booking

More booked consults from the traffic you already have

Today, Book a Free Consultation opens a contact form: name, email, phone, office and a message box, with no question about what the client wants. Schedule an Appointment Online opens the full Zenoti service menu in a new tab. On a phone, an event popup fills the screen before either. Four changes raise the share of visitors who book, starting in month 1.

1Start the form in the homepage heroThe purple button opens the first question right there, not a separate contact page.
2Ask about them, not the menuClients pick a goal like lines or sun damage, not one of 70+ treatments on a Zenoti list.
3Easy questions first, time lastGoal, then office, then contact details, then a time from your Zenoti calendar. At LaserAway, A/B tests showed easy questions first led more people to finish.
4One form everywhereThe same form on every treatment page and in place of the full-screen popup, so every visit has one clear next step.
