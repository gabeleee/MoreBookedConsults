Treatment + local pages

A page for every treatment at every spa

People search for a treatment and a place: “massage dallas”, “botox plano”, “couples massage houston”. Today Google can only rank three Hiatus treatment pages (Botox, Sculptra and Juvéderm). Massage, facials, HydraFacial and the rest live as cards on one long menu page, and their own pages are hidden from search. We give each treatment a real page first, then a local page for each spa: up to 168 pages, each written from that spa's real details and booking straight into that spa. The six treatments with the most searches go first.

Found where people searchEach page answers one treatment in one city or neighborhood, the way people type it.

Unique, not copiedReal local details on every page, so Google ranks each one instead of treating them as duplicates.

Booking

Start booking in one step, on hiatusspa.com

On a computer, “Book now” opens a list of fourteen locations, then leaves hiatusspa.com for the booking site, which asks for a category, a service, a visit type, a provider and a time before “Book Reservation”, where a picture puzzle can appear. On a phone, the header has no booking button, and the homepage's “Book Now” opens the day spa menu instead. Four changes shorten that path, starting in month 1.

1Choose the spa in one stepOne dropdown of all spas, grouped by city, asked at the end instead of the start. When we tested this at LaserAway, conversion held steady.
2Stay on hiatusspa.comNo handoff to an outside site. Bookings still land in Zenoti, so the front desk changes nothing.
3Easy questions first, time lastWhat they're coming in for and whether it's their first visit, then name, email and mobile, then the spa and time. In A/B tests I ran at LaserAway, easy questions first led more people to finish.
4Put it where people decideThe same form sits on every treatment and local page, and in the phone header, with the treatment or spa already chosen.
Treatment guides · months 3–6

Make Hiatus the name Texans find when they research a treatment

Before people book a first HydraFacial or a first Botox visit, they search questions: what it feels like, how long it lasts, what to do before and after. Hiatus has 11 blog posts today, and most are promotions. A topical map plans one clear guide per question, each linked to the treatment and local pages where people book.

This runs quietly in the background once the local pages are live. Your team's only job is approving pages.

One page per questionClear answers people and AI assistants can quote, instead of promotions that expire.
Every guide feeds a booking pageLinks run from each guide to the treatment and local pages where appointments are booked.
New pages rank fasterOnce a topic is fully covered, Google trusts new pages on it sooner, including the new local pages.
