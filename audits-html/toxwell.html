Consult booking

More booked consults from the traffic you already have

Today the homepage never links to your booking calendar: the main button is “Book via WhatsApp,” and the calendar hides behind a button called “Get In Touch.” Every one of your 102 services only takes online bookings 1 to 3 days ahead, and consultations named “free” ask for $50 online. Four changes raise the share of visitors who book, starting in month 1.

1Open the booking windowLet people book next week, not just in the next three days. It's one setting per service, and it's the fastest win on this page.
2Start the form in the homepage heroThe first question replaces “Book via WhatsApp” as the main button. WhatsApp stays, one tap lower.
3Easy questions first, time lastOne tap per question, then contact details, then real open times. At LaserAway, A/B tests showed easy questions first led more people to finish.
4No surprise at checkoutA consult named “free” shouldn't end at a $50 payment screen. We'll test a card on file or a clearly labeled deposit instead.
