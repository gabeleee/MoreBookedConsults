Booking

Make your New Guest special say what it is, and book

Your homepage's main button is “New Guest special”, but the page it opens never says what the special is. It's a form for name, email, phone and goal with no time to pick, and its text-message consent still names iCRYO. “Schedule now” leaves your site for Vagaro, where a new guest starts at an empty “Select Service” dropdown. Four changes fix that, starting in month 1.

1Name the offerSay what the New Guest special is on the button and in the form, so it pulls people in.
2Start with their goalYou already ask “What areas of your life would you like to improve?” Keep it, as one tap. In A/B tests I ran at LaserAway, easy questions first led more people to finish.
3Book a time on your siteContact details, then a time, booked into Vagaro. The front desk changes nothing.
4Text back in minutesAnyone who stops before picking a time gets a text from the LeadConnector system your site already runs on.
Topical authority · starts month 1

Become Chandler's answer on weight loss and recovery

Your site has 18 pages and no articles, so Google has little to go on beyond each service page. A topical map plans one clear guide per question people ask before they book, like what semaglutide costs, how many hyperbaric sessions it takes, or cryotherapy vs. an ice bath. Each guide links to the service page where they book.

This runs alongside the page build from month 1. Your team's only job is approving guides, with a medical review on anything clinical.
