When a site covers a topic completely, Google treats it as the authority: more of its pages rank, new ones rank faster, and more clients arrive from search. Consult booking

More booked consults from the traffic you already have

Today “Book” opens the full Boulevard service menu, so a first-time visitor has to know which treatment to pick. The $50 offer, the contact form and the chat each ask for a name and number but not what she wants done, and none of them report back to your analytics. Four changes raise the share of visitors who book, starting in month 1.

1Start the form in the homepage heroThe first question replaces the “Book appointment” button, so booking starts on the first screen.
2Ask about her, not the menu“What would you like to improve?” works for someone who doesn't know the difference between Dysport and Daxxify.
3Easy questions first, time lastOne tap per question, then contact details, then Boulevard's calendar, already on the right consultation. At LaserAway, A/B tests showed easy questions first led more people to finish.
4One form everywhereThe same form ends every treatment page and replaces the popup, with the $50 first-visit credit built in.
