Local pages

A page for every treatment at every clinic

People search for a treatment and a place: “laser hair removal alpharetta”, “lip filler atlanta”, “botox winter park”. AYA has a strong page for each treatment and a page for each clinic, but nothing that joins the two, so Google shows a general clinic page at best. Twelve clinics and eight core treatments make 96 local pages, each one written from that clinic's real details and booking straight into that clinic.

Booking

Start booking in one step, on ayaskin.com

Today every “Book Now” button opens a list of regions, then a list of clinics, then leaves ayaskin.com for an outside booking site that asks for a service, a time and a texted login code. Patients choose where to go twice before anyone asks what they want. Four changes shorten that path, starting in month 1.

1Choose the clinic in one stepOne dropdown of all twelve clinics, grouped by city, in place of two pages. When we tested it at LaserAway, conversion held steady.
2Stay on ayaskin.comNo handoff to an outside site. Bookings still land in Zenoti, so the front desk changes nothing.
3Easy questions first, time lastTwo quick questions about them first, then name, email and mobile, then the clinic and time. In A/B tests I ran at LaserAway, easy questions first led more people to finish.
4Put it where people decideThe same form sits on every treatment and local page, with the treatment or clinic already chosen.
