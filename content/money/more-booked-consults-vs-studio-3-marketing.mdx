More Booked Consults and Studio 3 Marketing both work with med spas and plastic surgery practices, and that's where the overlap ends.

Studio 3 Marketing is a large agency that can run your whole marketing stack, from the brand and the website to the ads and the follow-up.

More Booked Consults is one person doing three things: conversion rate optimization, local SEO and managed Meta ads.

So this isn't a choice between two similar vendors.

It's a choice between hiring a whole team and hiring a specialist, and the right answer depends on what your practice is missing.

## <span className="he">🧰</span> What do More Booked Consults and Studio 3 Marketing each offer?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/studio-3.webp" small="/images/agencies/studio-3-800.webp" alt="Studio 3 Marketing med spa page" width="1280" height="700" caption="studio3marketing.com/med-spa, September 2026" />

[Studio 3 Marketing](https://www.studio3marketing.com/med-spa/) was founded in 2011, and its [about page](https://www.studio3marketing.com/about/) says it has grown to 200+ employees with offices in Austin, Los Angeles and Tampa.

That page also says all work is produced in-house, "with no outsourcing and no templates," at a 1:4 employee-to-client ratio.

- **Marketing services:** branding, website design, video and photography, SEO, AI search optimization, digital advertising, email marketing and social media marketing.
- **Paid ads:** Google Ads plus Facebook and Instagram; it describes itself as a Google Premier Partner for five years running and a Meta Business Partner.
- **LeadLoop:** its own software that gathers leads in one dashboard, attributes each to its source, and records follow-up by email, phone, text and social DMs in one history.
- **Patient Acquisition Coaching:** a three-month program that reviews your intake and consult flow and gives staff and managers weekly feedback.
- **Multi-location technology and a content platform** for practices with several sites.

Studio 3 Marketing isn't aesthetics-only: its site also lists medical, legal and national brand clients.

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

More Booked Consults does three things, and only for aesthetic practices.

- **[Conversion rate optimization](/medspa-cro/):** one controlled test a month on the page or form that loses the most booked consults.
- **[Local SEO](/medspa-seo/):** treatment and city pages, Google Business Profile work and citations.
- **[Managed Meta ads](/medspa-advertising/):** 8–12 new static image ads a month, landing pages built for them, and one landing-page test a month.

It's run by Gabe Meierotto, who does the work himself, so there's no account manager between you and the person running your tests, pages and ads.

It doesn't run Google Ads, rebuild websites, set up CRMs or produce video ads, and Studio 3 Marketing does offer Google Ads, websites, video and lead software.

## <span className="he">💵</span> What do More Booked Consults and Studio 3 Marketing cost?

More Booked Consults publishes every price on its [pricing page](/pricing/).

| Service | Price | Minimum term |
|---|---|---|
| CRO | $2,500 a month | 6 months |
| Local SEO | $2,500 a month | 6 months |
| CRO + local SEO | $5,000 a month | 6 months |
| Managed Meta ads | $3,000 a month, plus $2,500 one-time setup | 3 months |

For Meta ads, you pay your ad spend directly to Meta (minimum $3,000 a month), and it's never marked up or charged as a percentage.

Studio 3 Marketing doesn't publish prices.

As of October 2026 we found no pricing, fees or minimum spend on its med spa, advertising, LeadLoop, coaching or about pages, and its [contact page](https://www.studio3marketing.com/contact/) offers a form and a phone number.

That's common for full-service agencies, because the price depends on which services you take.

It also means a like-for-like price comparison isn't possible: a Studio 3 Marketing proposal will usually cover far more than three services.

Ask for the monthly fee, any setup fee and how ad spend is paid, in writing, then compare it with what you'd spend covering the same scope another way.

## <span className="he">🔒</span> Who owns your accounts and data?

On ad accounts, the two companies say the same thing.

Studio 3 Marketing's [digital advertising page](https://www.studio3marketing.com/med-spa/services/digital-advertising/) says it "runs everything from your account so you retain control, visibility, and ownership."

More Booked Consults works as a partner inside your own Meta Business Manager, so the ad account, pixel and audiences stay yours.

With More Booked Consults, your website, domain, Google Business Profile and analytics also stay in your name, and the landing pages, ads, test results and reports are yours to keep when the engagement ends.

Studio 3 Marketing's site doesn't say who owns a website it designs, or how you export your lead history from LeadLoop if you leave.

Those are fair questions for any agency that builds your site and runs your lead software, so get the answers in writing.

Neither company publishes a Business Associate Agreement.

More Booked Consults says so on its fact sheet and asks practices that need one to raise it before sharing any patient data; we found no HIPAA or BAA statement on Studio 3 Marketing's site.

Read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before anyone installs tracking on your site.

## <span className="he">📈</span> What results does each publish?

Studio 3 Marketing publishes a [portfolio of client case studies](https://www.studio3marketing.com/our-work/); the numbers below are self-reported, and we haven't audited them.

- **Aurora Medical Spa:** +199% web traffic in six months and 2.5 million Instagram impressions in two months.
- **Its own metrics:** 58 #1 rankings in the top 200 US cities, per its med spa page.

More Booked Consults has no published client case studies yet, because it was founded in 2026.

What it publishes is its founder's record in a former in-house job.

As Director of CRO at LaserAway from 2018 to 2023, Gabe Meierotto ran a testing program that took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% across 2,600+ tested variations.

That's documented in [Intellimize's published case study](/laseraway-intellimize-case-study.pdf), and every number is sourced on the [results page](/results/).

Those are his personal results at one large brand, not client results of More Booked Consults, and they don't predict what your practice will see.

If a track record of agency clients matters most to you, Studio 3 Marketing has one and More Booked Consults doesn't yet.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Should you pick Studio 3 Marketing or More Booked Consults?

**Pick Studio 3 Marketing if:**

- You need a new brand, a new website, or photo and video, none of which More Booked Consults offers.
- You want Google Ads, email and social run by the same team as your SEO and Meta ads.
- You run several locations, or plan to, and want a large team and multi-location technology behind them.
- You want lead management software and front-desk coaching from your marketing agency.
- You want an agency that has been in business since 2011 and publishes client case studies.

**Pick More Booked Consults if:**

- You already get traffic and inquiries, and too few of them book.
- You want to rank in Google's map pack for your treatments, or want Meta ads with landing pages built and tested for you.
- You want prices, minimum terms and cancellation rules published before you talk to anyone.
- You'd rather work directly with one senior operator than with an account team.
- You're comfortable hiring a company founded in 2026 that has no client case studies yet.

Some practices will fit neither, and the [Studio 3 Marketing alternatives](/studio-3-marketing-alternatives/) page compares five other options.
