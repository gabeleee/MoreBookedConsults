A laser hair removal prospect has the same handful of questions every time.

<Checklist>

- Does it work on my skin and hair type?
- How many sessions will it take?
- What does a package cost, and can I pay over time?

A landing page that answers those in the right order, with a clear package offer and a short form, converts far better than a service page that makes the visitor hunt.
