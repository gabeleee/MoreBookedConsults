This page lays those out side by side, so you can pick the one that fits how your practice runs.

## <span className="he">💵</span> What do Studio 3 Marketing and Plastix Marketing cost?

Neither Studio 3 Marketing nor Plastix Marketing publishes prices, as of September 2026.

Studio 3 Marketing shows no pricing, fees or minimums on its med spa, advertising, coaching or about pages, and routes pricing questions to its contact form and phone line.

Plastix Marketing's Growth Plan page offers a 20-minute call instead of a price list, and its consultation form asks for your monthly marketing budget in ranges from under $5,000 to $20,000+ a month.

Plastix Marketing's terms say fees are set in each order: a monthly retainer, any paid-ads management fee, and any setup fees, with ad spend kept separate and paid to the platforms directly or funded through the agency.

Its advertising page adds that you will see your ad budget and its management fees, with "no hidden markups."

So the only fair way to answer "which is cheaper" is to get both proposals in writing and compare them on the same line items.

## <span className="he">📝</span> What contract terms do Studio 3 Marketing and Plastix Marketing publish?

Plastix Marketing publishes its [Master Terms of Service](https://plastixmarketing.com/terms/), last updated June 3, 2026.

- **Initial term:** 6 or 12 months, as set in your order, and 12 months if the order doesn't say.
- **After that:** month-to-month, and either side can cancel with 30 days' written notice.
- **Leaving early:** ending for convenience during the initial term costs 50% of the remaining monthly retainer fees.
- **Ad accounts:** you own them unless your order says otherwise; templates and account shells in Plastix Marketing's own business manager stay with it.
- **Your work:** custom work transfers to you on full payment, excluding Plastix Marketing's reusable templates and methods.
- **Hosting:** if you cancel hosting with Plastix Marketing, you have 48 hours to migrate the site.

Studio 3 Marketing doesn't publish a minimum term, notice period or termination fee on its site; its advertising page says ads run from your own account, but it doesn't say who owns a website it builds or your lead data.

That isn't a red flag on its own, but it means you learn Studio 3 Marketing's terms from the proposal, not before the call.

Whichever you pick, get the minimum term, notice period and who owns the website, domain, ad accounts and Google Business Profile in writing.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> Do Studio 3 Marketing and Plastix Marketing sign a BAA?

Plastix Marketing does, per its own terms: the parties sign a Business Associate Agreement before Plastix Marketing accesses any practice system containing protected health information.

Its hosting compliance page also describes HIPAA-compliant hosting with encryption at rest and in transit, and lists Business Associate Agreements.

We found no HIPAA or BAA statement on Studio 3 Marketing's med spa, about, advertising or LeadLoop pages, or in its privacy policy, in September 2026.

That doesn't mean Studio 3 Marketing won't sign one, so ask before any tracking, forms or lead software go live.

This matters because tracking pixels on lead forms can send patient health information to ad platforms; read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before either agency installs tracking on your site.

## <span className="he">🔗</span> Which tools does each agency own, and what happens to your data?

Studio 3 Marketing's LeadLoop is its own software.

It collects every lead in one dashboard, attributes each to its marketing source, and records follow-up by email, phone, text and social DMs in one history.

Studio 3 Marketing's site doesn't say how you export that data or what happens to it if you leave, so ask.

Plastix Marketing doesn't sell its own CRM.

It's a certified partner with HubSpot, Keap and SharpSpring, and its CRM support page says setup, implementation and maintenance are part of every Growth Plan, with integrations to your website, its reporting platform, and even an electronic health records API.

Because the CRM is a third-party product, the records can live in an account you control, but confirm whose name the account is in.

The trade-off is simple: software built by your agency can be tightly wired to its marketing, while a mainstream CRM is easier to keep if you change agencies.

## <span className="he">📈</span> What results do Studio 3 Marketing and Plastix Marketing publish?

All of the numbers below are self-reported by each agency, as shown on its site in September 2026, and we haven't audited them.

- **Studio 3 Marketing:** Aurora Medical Spa, +199% web traffic in six months (a client since 2024, per the case study); its about page also reports a 90% client retention rate.
- **Plastix Marketing:** Buckhead Plastic Surgery, a 164% lead increase (50 to 241 leads a month) and 3,500+ new leads over 18 months; LUX Med Spa, 400+ new leads a month; its fact sheet reports clients stay 3.2 years on average.

Neither agency publishes a date for every case study, so ask when the work happened and whether the same team would run your account.
