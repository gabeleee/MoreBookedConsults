## <span className="he">🧭</span> How to choose a plastic surgery marketing agency

Start with where your patients drop out, not with the list.

<Compare>

<Side title="You lose patients after they find you" tone="yes">

Inquiries arrive but consults don't get booked, or consults don't turn into surgeries.

A focused operator on [conversion](/plastic-surgery-cro/) and follow-up, or an agency with a CRM and intake coaching, will move revenue faster than more traffic.

</Side>

<Side title="Patients can't find you yet" tone="neutral">

Thin website, weak rankings, no ads, no before-and-after gallery worth browsing.

A full-service firm that builds the site, content, galleries and SEO together makes more sense here, and so does budgeting for it.

</Side>

</Compare>

Then compare your shortlist on the criteria below.

### Plastic surgery SEO and local visibility

Surgical patients search by procedure and city, so ask how the agency builds [procedure pages](/blog/plastic-surgery-procedure-pages-seo/) for rhinoplasty, breast augmentation, liposuction and facelifts, and how it works your [Google Business Profile](/blog/plastic-surgery-google-business-profile/).

Every agency on this list sells SEO; the difference is whether it's built around the procedures you actually want more of.

If SEO is the only thing you're hiring for, the [best plastic surgery SEO companies](/best-plastic-surgery-seo-companies/) comparison narrows the field to that one need.

### Google Ads vs Meta ads

Most of these agencies run Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads.

In our experience Google clicks for surgical searches are very expensive and Meta ads get aesthetic practices better results, which is why we run Meta only; see [plastic surgeon Google Ads](/blog/plastic-surgeon-google-ads/) for the other side of that trade-off.

### Content, social media and photography

Full-service firms earn their retainers on content in your voice, organic social, and photography and video.

Before-and-after galleries matter more in surgery than almost anywhere else, so ask who shoots, edits and publishes them, and read the [before-and-after photo guide](/blog/medspa-before-after-photos/) for the consent side.

Our view is that organic social is slow for most practices compared with paid ads, but plenty of surgeons value it for brand.

### Reviews and reputation

Patients shortlist surgeons on Google and RealSelf reviews, so ask whether review generation is included; see [RealSelf and Google reviews](/blog/plastic-surgery-reviews-realself/).

### Lead management, CRM and intake

Surgical decisions take months, so the follow-up between the first inquiry and the booked surgery is where most revenue leaks.

Ask how leads are tracked, who follows up, and whether the agency trains your coordinators; the [lead management guide](/blog/plastic-surgery-lead-management/) and [patient coordinator guide](/blog/patient-coordinator-consult-conversion/) cover what good looks like.

### Reporting and call tracking

The monthly report should show consults booked and surgeries scheduled by channel, and cost per booked consult.

That needs call tracking and a CRM connected to your calendar; see [attribution and call tracking](/blog/medspa-attribution-call-tracking/) and [patient acquisition cost](/blog/plastic-surgery-patient-acquisition-cost/).

### Compliance

Ask who signs a BAA, how tracking avoids sending health data to ad platforms, and how testimonials follow the FTC's endorsement rules.

Start with [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/), [FTC testimonial rules](/blog/ftc-testimonial-rules-medspa/) and [aesthetic advertising laws](/blog/medspa-marketing-laws/).

### Proprietary tools and lock-in

Several agencies here run their own CRM, gallery or website platform.

That can be a real advantage, but ask what happens to your data, website and ad accounts if you leave.

### Pricing, terms and the first 90 days

Get the retainer, the ad-spend arrangement, the minimum term and the notice period in writing, and ask what the agency will ship in the first 90 days.

Published terms here range from a 12-month minimum on monthly services to our 6-month minimum on CRO and SEO; the [plastic surgery marketing cost guide](/plastic-surgery-marketing-cost/) breaks down typical line items, and our prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing/).

## <span className="he">🚫</span> Mistakes to avoid when hiring an agency

<Checklist>

- Trusting a "best agencies" list without checking who wrote it.
- Accepting ranking or patient-volume guarantees; nobody controls Google or your market.
- Hiring a generalist who treats a $10,000 procedure like a $99 facial.
- Judging the agency on traffic or cost per lead instead of booked consults.
- Signing without knowing the minimum term, notice period, and who owns the website and ad accounts.
- Asking for market exclusivity without asking what it costs and whether it's in the contract.

</Checklist>
