Growth99 and More Booked Consults both work with aesthetic practices, and that's most of what they share.

Growth99 sells software and marketing together: your CRM, texting, review requests, forms and AI front desk live in its application, and its team builds your website and runs SEO and ads.

More Booked Consults sells no software at all.

It's one person working inside the site, ad account and Google Business Profile you already own, on three things: conversion, local SEO and Meta ads.

So the real question isn't which is better, it's whether your gap is missing tools or traffic that doesn't book.

## <span className="he">🧰</span> What do More Booked Consults and Growth99 each offer?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/growth99.webp" small="/images/agencies/growth99-800.webp" alt="Growth99 med spa marketing page" width="1280" height="700" caption="growth99.com, September 2026" />

[Growth99](https://growth99.com/speciality/med-spa/) was founded in 2017 and says it began as a marketing agency and became a full platform for aesthetic and elective wellness practices.

Its industry list also includes dental practices and salons, and it says it supports 1,000+ practices (self-reported).

- **Essentials plan:** email and SMS automation, a patient self-assessment tool, an AI chatbot, a form builder and reporting.
- **Accelerator plan:** adds a review generator, an in-app social media manager, a custom website of up to 15 pages with hosting and unlimited updates, and landing page templates inside its app.
- **Elite plan:** adds full SEO with generative search optimization, monthly backlinking, one blog post a month, a website of up to 25 pages and a dedicated account manager.
- **Paid media add-on:** Google Ads and Meta in three tiers that manage up to $1,500, $3,500 or $8,000 a month in ad spend, each with landing pages and call tracking.
- **Gia:** an AI front desk assistant that Growth99 says replies to inbound leads in under 60 seconds across texts, forms, Facebook, Instagram and phone.

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

More Booked Consults was founded in 2026 by Gabe Meierotto, Director of CRO at LaserAway from 2018 to 2023, and works only with aesthetic practices.

- **[Conversion rate optimization](/medspa-cro/):** one controlled test a month on the page or form that loses the most booked consults.
- **[Local SEO](/medspa-seo/):** treatment and city pages, Google Business Profile work and citations.
- **[Managed Meta ads](/medspa-advertising/):** 8–12 new static image ads a month, landing pages built for them, and one landing-page test a month.
- **First 30 days:** a multi-step consult form built and live on your site, and local SEO work started.
- **Not offered:** Google Ads, website rebuilds, CRM setup, video ads, per-lead pricing.

Everything in Growth99's software column (CRM, texting, review requests, chatbot, AI front desk) is something More Booked Consults doesn't have.

## <span className="he">💵</span> What do More Booked Consults and Growth99 cost?

More Booked Consults publishes every price on its [pricing page](/pricing/).

CRO is $2,500 a month, local SEO is $2,500 a month, and the two together are $5,000 a month.

Managed Meta ads are $3,000 a month plus a one-time $2,500 setup.

You pay the ad spend directly to Meta, with a $3,000 a month minimum, and it's never marked up or charged as a percentage.

That makes the minimum commitment easy to work out: $15,000 for six months of CRO or local SEO, or $11,500 in fees for three months of ads, before ad spend.

Growth99's [pricing page](https://growth99.com/pricing-plans/) lists every plan inclusion in detail, but each plan and paid media tier reads "Pricing available upon request."

So you can't compare the two on price from their websites alone.

Ask Growth99 for the monthly fee, any setup cost, how ad spend is billed and the term, in writing.

The [med spa marketing cost guide](/medspa-marketing-cost/) shows what typical retainers cover.

More Booked Consults' minimums are still commitments, so don't sign either one planning to leave in month two.

## <span className="he">🔒</span> Who owns your accounts, pages and data?

With More Booked Consults, the practice owns its accounts from the first day, as the [fact sheet](/fact-sheet/) spells out.

The Meta ad account, pixel and audiences sit in your own Business Manager, and the website, domain, Google Business Profile and analytics stay in your name.

Landing pages, ads, test results and reports built during the engagement are yours to keep when it ends.

There's also no software to migrate off, because More Booked Consults doesn't have any.

Growth99's terms say it keeps ownership of its software tools, templates and reusable code, and that front-facing website deliverables named in your service order transfer to you once paid in full.

The landing pages in its plans are described as pre-designed templates inside the Growth99 application.

Its terms say it's your responsibility to download your content before termination, and that it keeps patient health information for at least 60 days afterwards.

We couldn't find a statement in Growth99's published terms on who holds the ad accounts, so ask before you sign.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> Does either one sign a BAA?

Growth99 publishes a full [Business Associate Agreement](https://growth99.com/baa/), and its service terms reference it.

More Booked Consults doesn't publish a BAA, so if you need one, ask before sharing any patient data.

That's a real point in Growth99's favor, and it follows from the model: a CRM and texting tool holds patient records, and a consultancy working on pages and ads mostly doesn't.

A BAA covers how a vendor handles patient data, not whether your tracking pixels send health data to ad platforms.

Read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before anyone installs tracking on your site.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Should you pick More Booked Consults or Growth99?

**Pick Growth99 if:**

- You need a CRM, email and SMS automation, review requests or an AI front desk, and want them in one login.
- You need a new website, Google Ads or social media graphics, none of which More Booked Consults offers.
- You need a published BAA from your marketing vendor.
- Your ad budget is under $3,000 a month, the minimum spend for More Booked Consults' managed ads; Growth99's entry tier manages up to $1,500 a month.
- You want a team, an account manager and published client stories, not a one-person company founded in 2026.

**Pick More Booked Consults if:**

- You already have a CRM and booking software you like, and don't want to move your front office into a new platform.
- Your gap is conversion, map-pack visibility or Meta ads, not missing tools.
- You want to see the price and the terms before any sales call.
- You'd rather commit to 6 or 3 months, then month to month, than to a one-year term that renews.
- You want the person you talk to and the person doing the work to be the same person.

Some practices could reasonably use both, since Growth99's software and More Booked Consults' three services barely overlap.
