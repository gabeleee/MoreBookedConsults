Etna Interactive and Rosemont Media are two of the longest-running website and SEO firms that plastic surgeons shortlist.

Both build custom WordPress websites, both sell SEO with content and link building, and both run Google and social ads.

The differences are in how each is packaged: what they publish about price, what software they own, how the engagement is structured, and which kind of practice each one is built around.

This page lays those out side by side so you can pick the one that fits how your practice runs.

## <span className="he">💵</span> How much do Etna Interactive and Rosemont Media cost?

Rosemont Media is the more transparent of the two on its core services.

Rosemont Media's plastic surgery website page says custom website packages typically range from $10,000 to $40,000, with flexible payment options.

Rosemont Media's plastic surgeon SEO page says monthly SEO typically ranges from $1,000 to $3,000, scaled to your market's competitiveness.

Etna Interactive does not publish its retainer or website prices.

Etna Interactive does publish prices for its products: Core Lead Management $60/month, Call Tracking from $60/month per location, Curator B&A $140/month, Reputation Ally from $195/month per location, all with install fees not included.

Etna Interactive's Core page says Core is a standard feature in every Etna marketing plan.

Spotlight Photo+Video, Etna Interactive's on-site shoot, starts at $5,700 for the day plus transportation, lodging and meals for two.

Neither agency publishes a management fee for ads, so ask how ad spend is billed and whether it is marked up.

The [plastic surgery marketing cost guide](/plastic-surgery-marketing-cost/) shows what typical line items look like across agencies.

## <span className="he">📝</span> What contract terms do Etna Interactive and Rosemont Media publish?

Neither agency publishes a minimum term, a notice period or an early-termination fee.

Etna Interactive's What to Expect page describes the process: a free assessment call of about an hour, a written proposal within a week, and work that starts after it receives your signed proposal and deposit.

The same page describes monthly reporting, a strategy meeting every six months and yearly goal setting, and says you may adjust your level of service or move to a different vendor.

Rosemont Media publishes no contract terms; its about page says it works with one practice per market and gives each client a single point of contact.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> Do Etna Interactive and Rosemont Media sign a BAA?

Neither publishes Business Associate Agreement terms, as of September 2026.

Etna Interactive describes its Core lead management system and Reputation Ally review processing as HIPAA-compliant.

Rosemont Media's consulting page says compliance with HIPAA guidelines and FTC rules on testimonials and before-and-after images is built into its work.

That still leaves the question every practice should ask: tracking pixels on forms and procedure pages can send patient data to ad platforms, so read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) and ask either agency for a signed BAA before tracking goes live.

## <span className="he">🗂️</span> Who owns the website, data and tools?

Both agencies say you own your website.

Etna Interactive builds on WordPress with its own custom themes and plugins, and its website page says your WordPress website is entirely portable.

Etna Interactive's What to Expect page says that if you part ways, you own all your assets.

Etna Interactive's products (Core, Curator B&A, Reputation Ally, Call Tracking and the SILO for WordPress plugin) are its own software, and we found no published terms on exporting data from them.

Rosemont Media's plastic surgery website page says you retain full ownership of your website's code and content, with no leasing or hidden lock-ins.

Rosemont Media also offers WordPress-optimized cloud hosting and ongoing maintenance.

With either agency, ask in writing how you'd export your galleries, lead history and call-tracking numbers, and whose name the domain and ad accounts are in.

Camp Plastic Surgery reported revenue more than tripled and website traffic up 735% since 2016.

Rosemont Media publishes no quantified plastic surgery case study that we found.

Rosemont Media shows a portfolio of custom surgeon websites and says it keeps a client retention rate of over 90 percent (self-reported).

We haven't audited either agency's numbers.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Should you pick Etna Interactive or Rosemont Media?

**Pick Etna Interactive if:**

- You want one long-term partner for website, SEO, ads, email, reviews and reporting, and you plan to stay for years.
- You want lead tracking, gallery software and call tracking from the agency itself rather than separate vendors.
- Your front desk or coordinators need training on turning inquiries into consultations; see our [lead management guide](/blog/plastic-surgery-lead-management/) for what that covers.

**Pick Rosemont Media if:**

- Your main project is a new custom website with [before-and-after galleries](/blog/plastic-surgery-before-after-galleries/), followed by ongoing SEO.
- You want published price ranges before you get on a call.
- Market exclusivity matters to you, since Rosemont Media takes one practice per market.

Etna Interactive's own about page is candid that it may not be the right partner if low price is your main criterion or you see online marketing as a short-term pursuit.

## <span className="he">📋</span> Questions to ask both before you sign

<Checklist>

- What is the minimum term, the notice period and any early-termination fee?
- What will the website, the monthly retainer, setup and ad management each cost, in writing?
- Is ad spend billed through you, marked up, or charged as a percentage?
- Will you sign a BAA, and how does your tracking keep health data away from ad platforms?
- Who owns the domain, hosting, galleries, lead history, call-tracking numbers and ad accounts if I leave?
- Does the monthly report show booked consults and surgeries by channel, not just traffic and rankings?
