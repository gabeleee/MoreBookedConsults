## <span className="he">✅</span> What does Studio 3 Marketing do well?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/studio-3.webp" small="/images/agencies/studio-3-800.webp" alt="Studio 3 Marketing med spa page" width="1280" height="700" caption="studio3marketing.com/med-spa, September 2026" />

[Studio 3 Marketing](https://www.studio3marketing.com/med-spa/) was founded in 2011, lists its national HQ in Austin, Texas, with offices in Los Angeles and Tampa, and its about page says it has grown to 200+ employees.

A fair summary of its strengths, from its own site:

- **Everything in-house:** branding, websites, photo and video, SEO, AI search optimization, Google and Meta ads, email and social, with "no outsourcing and no templates" according to its about page.
- **Lead management software:** LeadLoop gathers leads in one dashboard, attributes each to its marketing source, and handles follow-up by email, call, text and social DMs.
- **Intake coaching:** a Patient Acquisition Coaching program aimed at getting more bookings from existing leads.
- **Staffing depth:** it describes a 1:4 employee-to-client ratio, and calls itself a Google Premier Partner and Meta Business Partner.
- **Proof (self-reported):** Aurora Medical Spa, +199% web traffic in six months.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Why do practices look for a Studio 3 Marketing alternative?

Most of the reasons are about fit, not quality.

- **No published pricing:** you can't compare Studio 3 Marketing's cost before a sales call, and some owners want a number first.
- **Contract terms unknown up front:** its site doesn't publish a minimum term or notice period.
- **Scope wider than the gap:** a full brand, content and media team is more than a practice needs when the real problem is conversion or map-pack visibility.
- **A different model:** some owners would rather hire a specialist for one channel, or an ads-first agency on a short minimum, than retain a large agency for everything.
- **A BAA in writing:** we found no HIPAA or BAA statement on its site, and some owners want one before any tracking goes live.

## <span className="he">📋</span> Questions to ask any agency before you switch

<Checklist>

- What is the minimum term, the notice period, and any early-termination fee?
- Who owns the ad accounts, the website, the domain and the Google Business Profile, and are they in my name today?
- Will you sign a BAA, and how does your tracking avoid sending health data to ad platforms?
- Does your monthly report show booked consults by channel, not just leads and clicks?
- What happens to the landing pages, integrations and automations you build if I leave?
- Do you mark up ad spend or charge a percentage of it?

</Checklist>

Ask the outgoing agency the same ownership questions before you give notice.
