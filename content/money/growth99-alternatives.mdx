Growth99 sells two things at once: its own practice software (CRM, email and SMS automation, reviews, forms, an AI front desk) and done-for-you marketing (websites, SEO, Google and Meta ads).

That bundle comes on a one-year term that renews automatically, according to its published terms.

So the natural alternatives split in two.

If you want the marketing without depending on a vendor's software, you want an agency that works in your own accounts and tools.

If you mostly want the software, you want software without the agency, and your [booking software](/blog/medspa-booking-software/) may already cover part of it.

This page covers the first group: five agencies worth weighing against Growth99, each with a clear reason to pick it.

## <span className="he">✅</span> What does Growth99 do well?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/growth99.webp" small="/images/agencies/growth99-800.webp" alt="Growth99 med spa marketing page" width="1280" height="700" caption="growth99.com, September 2026" />

Growth99 has focused on aesthetic and elective wellness practices since 2017, and says it supports more than 1,000 practices (self-reported).

- **One login for the front office:** email and SMS automation, a form builder, a patient self-assessment tool and an AI chatbot come in every plan, with review requests and a custom website from the Accelerator plan up.
- **Detailed plan inclusions:** its pricing page spells out what each plan and paid media tier includes, down to managed ad spend caps of $1,500, $3,500 or $8,000 a month.
- **Both ad platforms:** its paid media tiers run Google Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, with landing pages and call tracking.
- **Speed to lead:** Gia, its AI front desk launched in 2026, is described as responding to every inbound inquiry in under 60 seconds.
- **A published BAA:** the full Business Associate Agreement is on its site.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> The 5 best Growth99 alternatives

### More Booked Consults: best for conversion, local SEO and Meta ads with no software to depend on

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

More Booked Consults is a med spa and plastic surgery marketing agency focused on conversion (landing pages, CRO), local SEO and managed Meta ads.

It's run by Gabe Meierotto, LaserAway's in-house Director of CRO from 2018 to 2023, and works only with aesthetic practices.

- **Services:** conversion-focused landing pages, [conversion rate optimization](/medspa-cro/), [local SEO](/medspa-seo/) built on topical authority (including Google Business Profile work and citation cleanup), and [managed Meta ads](/medspa-advertising/) with fresh static image creative every month.
- **Pricing and contract (published):** CRO $2,500/month and local SEO $2,500/month (both for $5,000/month), each on a 6-month minimum; managed Meta ads $3,000/month plus a $2,500 one-time setup on a 3-month minimum, with ad spend (minimum $3,000/month) paid by you directly to Meta and never marked up.
- **No software lock-in:** the work runs in your own site, ad account and Google Business Profile; there's no proprietary CRM or app to migrate off.
- **Not offered:** Google Ads, website rebuilds, organic social, email and SMS, CRM setup, review campaigns, photo or video.

At LaserAway, Gabe's testing program took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% across 2,600+ tested variations (documented in [Intellimize's published case study](/laseraway-intellimize-case-study.pdf); see [results](/results/)); those are his results in a former in-house role, not agency client results.

**Choose it over Growth99 if** you already have a CRM and booking software you're happy with, and your gap is a site that doesn't turn visitors into booked consults, weak map-pack visibility, or Meta ads that need pages built to convert.

**Choose it over Growth99 if** you want the same breadth (SEO, ads, automation, follow-up) but on a mainstream CRM you pick, rather than an agency's own platform.

Growth99 is the better choice when you want your CRM, texting, review requests, website and Google Ads under one login and one vendor.

It's also the better choice if you'd rather not assemble separate tools, and a one-year term is fine for you.

If your gap is conversion, start with how your [landing pages](/blog/medspa-landing-pages/) and site turn visitors into booked consults.

In our experience, Google clicks for med spa searches are very expensive and Meta ads get med spas better results, which is why we run Meta only (see [SEO vs Google Ads](/blog/medspa-seo-vs-google-ads/)).

## <span className="he">📝</span> Questions to ask any agency before you switch

Get these answered in writing before you sign anything new, whichever agency you pick.

<Checklist>

- What are the exit terms: minimum term, renewal, notice period, and what's owed if we end early?
- Who owns the ad accounts, the website, the domain and the Google Business Profile, and are they in my name today?
- Will you sign a BAA, and how does your tracking avoid sending patient health data to ad platforms?
- Will your monthly report show booked consults by channel, not just leads and clicks?
- What happens to my landing pages, forms and automations if I leave: do I keep them, or do they live in your software?
- Can I export my contacts, lead history and reviews before I give notice?

</Checklist>

Tracking pixels on lead forms can leak patient information, so read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before any new agency installs tracking on your site.
