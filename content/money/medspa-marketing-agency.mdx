## <span className="he">🖥️</span> Conversion-focused med spa website pages, not a redesign

Almost every med spa marketing company leads with web design, and they're right that the website is where bookings are won or lost.

Where we differ is the fix: instead of a full redesign that resets everything, we build the pages that sell your treatments and test the parts of your site that leak.

A redesign replaces what works along with what doesn't, and you rarely learn which was which.

What that looks like in practice:

## <span className="he">📍</span> Local SEO, Google Business Profile, and AI search

Local SEO is how the right people find you in the first place: ranking in the Google map pack and for the treatments your city searches.

Our approach is built on topical maps, which means covering every treatment, concern, and question your patients search, so Google sees your site as the local authority.

It also includes [Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/medspa-google-business-profile/) and [citation cleanup](/blog/medspa-citations-directories/), so your name, address, and phone number match across every directory.

We don't run review-generation campaigns.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> HIPAA and tracking pixels

Any medical spa digital marketing agency that installs tracking should know this risk: pixels and analytics can send data tying a person to a treatment or to being a patient to third-party ad platforms.

That can be an impermissible disclosure of protected health information under HIPAA.

We set tracking up with that risk in mind and collect less when in doubt, but we don't claim a certified HIPAA-compliant setup, and your compliance advisor should review yours.

The details are in our guide to [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/).

## <span className="he">⚖️</span> Specialist vs. generalist marketing agency

For a med spa owner, the question isn't "who offers the most services."

It's "who will actually move my booking rate," and on that question fewer levers, pulled by someone who's pulled them before, wins.

| | Specialist (More Booked Consults) | Full-service generalist | DIY |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who does the work | One senior operator | A team, often junior, across many clients | You or your staff |
| Services | Landing pages, CRO, local SEO, Meta ads | SEO, PPC, social, email, web, branding | Whatever you have time for |
| Measured by | Booked consults | Often reach, traffic, or reports | Your own tracking |
| Pricing | Flat monthly, no ad-spend markup | Retainer, sometimes a % of ad spend | Your time |
| Best for | Owners who want bookings, not activity | Owners who want one vendor for everything | Owners with time to learn |

The trade-off is real and worth naming: a specialist won't run your Google Ads or manage your social media.
