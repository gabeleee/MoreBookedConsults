Growth99 and Plastix Marketing both focus on aesthetic practices, both run SEO, websites and Google and Meta ads, and both automate follow-up.

The real difference is where your front office lives.

With Growth99, your CRM, texting, review requests, forms and AI front desk live in Growth99's own software.

With Plastix Marketing, an agency team runs the marketing and sets up a mainstream CRM (HubSpot, Keap or SharpSpring) that it connects to your website and reporting.

That choice drives most of the other differences: lock-in, what you keep if you leave, and how you'll work day to day.

## <span className="he">💵</span> What do Growth99 and Plastix Marketing cost?

Neither company publishes a price.

Growth99's [pricing page](https://growth99.com/pricing-plans/) lists every plan inclusion in detail, but every plan and add-on reads "Pricing available upon request."

Plastix Marketing has no pricing page; its Growth Plan page asks you to book a 20-minute call, and its form asks your monthly marketing budget (from under $5,000 to $20,000+ a month).

Plastix Marketing's terms say fees are set in each order: a monthly retainer, any paid-ads management fee, setup fees and add-ons.

Ask both for the fee, the setup cost, how ad spend is paid and the minimum term, in writing, on the same terms.

## <span className="he">🔒</span> Who owns your website, ad accounts and data?

This is where the platform-versus-agency split matters most.

Growth99's terms say it keeps ownership of its software tools, templates and reusable code, and that front-facing website deliverables named in your service order transfer to you once paid in full.

The landing pages in its plans are described as pre-designed templates inside the Growth99 application.

Its terms say it's your responsibility to download your content before termination, and that it keeps patient health information for at least 60 days afterwards.

Plastix Marketing's terms use a three-layer model: custom work made for you becomes yours on full payment, while its reusable templates and frameworks stay with Plastix under a license to you.

Its terms say you own your ad accounts unless the order says otherwise, and that accounts it builds inside its own business manager stay with it, with your campaign data exported on request.

Because the CRM Plastix Marketing sets up is HubSpot, Keap or SharpSpring, your contact records sit in a mainstream tool rather than in an agency's own software.

If Plastix Marketing hosts your site, its terms give you 48 hours after cancelling hosting to move it.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> Do Growth99 and Plastix Marketing sign a BAA?

Yes, both publish BAA terms.

Growth99 publishes a full [Business Associate Agreement](https://growth99.com/baa/), and its service agreement references it.

Plastix Marketing's terms say it acts as a Business Associate when its work needs access to a practice system containing patient health information, and that a BAA is signed before that access is given.

A BAA covers how a vendor handles patient data, not whether your tracking pixels send health data to ad platforms.

Read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before either company installs tracking on your site.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Should you pick Growth99 or Plastix Marketing?

**Pick Growth99 if:**

- You want your CRM, texting, review requests, forms and an AI front desk in one vendor's software, under one login.
- You'd rather choose from published plan inclusions and add-on tiers than scope a custom plan.
- A one-year term that renews automatically is fine for you, and you'll calendar the 60-day notice date.

**Pick Plastix Marketing if:**

- You want one aesthetics-only team running SEO, content, ads, social, video and email as one connected plan.
- You want a mainstream CRM (HubSpot, Keap or SharpSpring) set up and wired to your website and health records.
- You can commit to 6 or 12 months and aren't shopping on price, which Plastix Marketing itself says isn't its fit.

<Checklist>

- What is the total monthly fee, setup fee and ad-spend arrangement, in writing?
- What is the minimum term, renewal rule, notice period and early-exit cost?
- Which website, landing pages and automations do I keep if I leave?
- Are the ad accounts, domain and Google Business Profile in my name today?
- Can I export my contacts and lead history, and in what format?
- Will you sign a BAA before touching patient data, and how does your tracking avoid sending it to ad platforms?

</Checklist>
