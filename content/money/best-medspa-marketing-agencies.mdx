## <span className="he">🧭</span> How to choose the right med spa marketing agency

Start with your biggest gap, not with the list.

<Compare>

<Side title="Your gap is conversion or local visibility" tone="yes">

Traffic and inquiries arrive, but bookings don't follow, or you don't show in the map pack.

A focused operator on [CRO](/medspa-cro/) and [local SEO](/medspa-seo/) usually fixes more, for less, than a full-service retainer.

</Side>

<Side title="Your gap is the whole stack" tone="neutral">

No brand, no site worth keeping, nobody running ads, social or follow-up.

A full-service agency or an all-in-one platform makes more sense, and so does budgeting for it.

</Side>

</Compare>

Then compare shortlisted agencies on the criteria below.

### Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Most med spa patients choose from the Google map pack, so ask how the agency handles your [Google Business Profile](/blog/medspa-google-business-profile/), citations and location pages.

Every agency on this list offers SEO; the difference is whether it's built around the treatments and cities you actually sell.

If SEO is the only thing you're hiring for, the [best med spa SEO companies](/best-med-spa-seo-companies/) comparison narrows the field to that one need.

### Google Ads vs Meta ads

Nearly all of these agencies run both Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads.

In our experience, Google clicks for med spa searches are very expensive and Meta ads get med spas better results, which is why we run Meta only; the [SEO vs Google Ads](/blog/medspa-seo-vs-google-ads/) guide lays out the trade-off.

Whoever runs your ads should know Meta's health and wellness rules and the prescription drug rules that apply to [Botox advertising](/botox-lead-generation/).

If Meta ads are the only thing you want run, the [best med spa Facebook ads agencies](/best-med-spa-facebook-ads-agencies/) comparison narrows this list to that one job.

### A conversion-focused website

A new website is not the same as a website that books consults.

Ask how the agency tests pages, what it changes after launch, and whether paid traffic goes to [landing pages](/blog/medspa-landing-pages/) built for one offer.

### Reviews, social, email and SMS

Review generation, organic social, email and SMS, CRM automation, AI receptionists and photo or video production are where full-service agencies and platforms earn their retainers.

They're worth paying for if you'll use them; More Booked Consults doesn't offer them, and our view is that organic social is slow for most med spas compared with paid ads.

If reviews are your gap, start with the [review guide](/blog/medspa-get-more-reviews/).

### AI search

Several agencies now sell answer engine or generative engine optimization (AEO/GEO) for ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews.

In our experience, visibility there mostly follows from strong topical SEO, so judge it as part of the SEO work rather than a separate line item.

### Reporting

The report should show booked consults and revenue by channel, not just clicks and followers.

Call tracking and form tracking make that possible; see [attribution and call tracking](/blog/medspa-attribution-call-tracking/).

### HIPAA and BAAs

Tracking pixels and lead forms can send patient health information to ad platforms.

Ask who signs a BAA, and read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before any agency installs tracking on your site.

### Pricing and contracts

Get the retainer, the ad-spend arrangement, the minimum term and the notice period in writing.

The [med spa marketing cost guide](/medspa-marketing-cost/) shows what typical retainers cover, and our own prices are on the [pricing page](/pricing/).
