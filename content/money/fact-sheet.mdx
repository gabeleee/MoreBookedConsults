## <span className="he">🎯</span> What does More Booked Consults do?

More Booked Consults does three things, and only for aesthetic practices.

<Cards>

<Card icon="funnel" title="Conversion (CRO)">

One controlled test a month on the page or form that loses the most booked consults, run long enough to trust the result. [How CRO works](/medspa-cro/).

</Card>

<Card icon="pin" title="Local SEO">

Treatment and city pages, Google Business Profile work, and citations, so the practice shows up when patients nearby search. [How local SEO works](/medspa-seo/).

</Card>

8–12 new static image ads a month, landing pages built for them, and one landing-page test a month. [How managed ads work](/medspa-advertising/).

## <span className="he">💵</span> What does More Booked Consults cost?

Every price is published on the [pricing page](/pricing/).

| Service | Price | Minimum term |
|---|---|---|
| CRO | $2,500 a month | 6 months |
| Local SEO | $2,500 a month | 6 months |
| CRO + local SEO | $5,000 a month | 6 months |
| Managed Meta ads | $3,000 a month, plus $2,500 one-time setup | 3 months |

For Meta ads, the practice pays its ad spend directly to Meta, with a $3,000 a month minimum.

The spend is never marked up, and the fee is never a percentage of it.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> How do you cancel, and who owns the accounts?

After the minimum term, every service continues month to month.

To cancel, email hello@morebookedconsults.com with 30 days' notice.

There is no cancellation fee, and the final month's work and report are delivered as usual.

The practice owns its accounts from the first day:

- The Meta ad account, pixel, and audiences sit in the practice's own Business Manager, and More Booked Consults works inside it as a partner.
- The website, domain, Google Business Profile, and analytics stay in the practice's name.
- Landing pages, ads, test results, and reports built during the engagement are the practice's to keep when it ends.

## <span className="he">👤</span> Who runs More Booked Consults?

More Booked Consults is Gabe Meierotto.

He was Director of CRO at [LaserAway](https://www.laseraway.com) from 2018 to 2023.

In that role he took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% and tested more than 2,600 variations.

Those results are his own, from that job, and they aren't client results of More Booked Consults.

The sources for each number are on the [results page](/results/).

He does the work himself, so the person a practice talks to is the person running its tests, pages, and ads.
