## <span className="he">🎯</span> What does More Booked Consults do?

More Booked Consults sells one plan, the Optimized Booking System™, and it is built only for aesthetic practices.

For $499 a month it includes nine systems, all done for the practice:

| System | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Authority Atlas™](/local-seo/) | 20 new Google pages a month (treatment, cost and nearby-town pages), plus Google Business Profile work |
| [Glide Path™](/booking-systems/#glide-path) | A step-by-step booking flow that hands bookings to the practice's booking app (Boulevard, Vagaro, Mangomint, Zenoti, Aesthetic Record, GlossGenius and others) |
| [Sienna AI™](/sienna-ai/) | An AI receptionist that answers calls 24/7, calls new website leads back within 60 seconds, texts the booking link, never gives medical advice and hands off to staff |
| [Lift Lab™](/lift-lab/) | 2 design tests a month on the site, and the winners stay |
| [Second Glance™](/booking-systems/#second-glance) | An exit offer for visitors who are about to leave |
| [Consult Magnet™](/consult-magnet/) | A named first-visit offer for the practice's top treatment |
| [Leak Finder™](/booking-systems/#leak-finder) | Drop-off tracking on every booking step |
| [Rave Loop™](/rave-loop/) | A Google review request texted to every client after each visit |
| [Clear View™](/booking-systems/#clear-view) | A monthly plain-numbers report and a live dashboard |

Hosting is included too.

If the practice needs a new website, it is built free on the practice's own domain, with a 12-month minimum for the free build.

The website is optional, and most of the plan works on the site a practice already has.

Managed Meta ads (Spotlight™ Ads) are a separate add-on, not part of the $499 plan: static image ads, a flat monthly fee quoted on the demo, and a 3-month minimum.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> How do you cancel, and who owns what?

The plan is month to month.

To cancel, email hello@morebookedconsults.com with 30 days' notice, after any minimum that applies (12 months for a free website build, 3 months for Spotlight™ Ads).

There is no cancellation fee.

What the practice keeps:

- The website, domain and every page built stay the practice's.
- The Meta ad account, pixel, Google Business Profile and analytics are the practice's own.

What switches off on cancel:

- Sienna AI™, Rave Loop™ and the live dashboard run on More Booked Consults' system, so they stop when the plan ends.

## <span className="he">✅</span> What does More Booked Consults guarantee?

It makes two guarantees and no other promises.

- **Booking rate up in 90 days**, or More Booked Consults keeps working free (measured against the week-1 baseline, for sites with 300+ visitors a month).
- **Booking flow live in 14 days**, or month one is free.
