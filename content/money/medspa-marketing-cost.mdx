## <span className="he">🧮</span> How much should a med spa spend on marketing?

The right number depends on your revenue, your stage, and above all what a patient is worth to your business.

The starting benchmarks by practice stage, and how to set a budget from lifetime value, are in [how much med spas spend on marketing](/blog/medspa-marketing-budget/).

Two rules hold whatever number you pick.

<Callout type="warning">

Spending more on a funnel that leaks just wastes more, faster.

Fix conversion first, then size the budget, then scale what books.

</Callout>

The second rule: always ask whether a quote includes ad spend, because a management fee and an all-in marketing budget are very different numbers.

## <span className="he">🎯</span> The number that actually matters

<Callout type="key">

A cheap program that books nothing is expensive. A pricier one that books consistently is cheap.

So judge marketing on cost per booked consult: everything you spend in a month, divided by the consults it booked.

</Callout>

The fastest way to lower it usually isn't a cheaper retainer.

It's a higher booking rate on the traffic you already pay for.

## <span className="he">💎</span> What a booked consult is worth

A consult's value isn't the first treatment.

A Botox patient who comes back for maintenance, adds dermal fillers, or later books a CoolSculpting series is worth far more than their first visit.

That's patient lifetime value (LTV), and it's what cost per booked consult should be measured against.

The math is in [patient lifetime value](/blog/medspa-patient-lifetime-value/), and [client retention](/blog/medspa-client-retention/) covers how to keep patients coming back.

For treatment-level economics, see [Botox cost per lead](/blog/botox-cost-per-lead/), [dermal filler marketing](/blog/dermal-filler-marketing/), and [CoolSculpting marketing](/blog/coolsculpting-marketing/).

## <span className="he">📏</span> Metrics that show whether spend is working

Every step between an ad and an attended consult has its own metric, and each is a place spend can leak.

<Funnel title="Where cost per consult is decided" caption="Illustrative shape, not benchmark rates. Each step shows the metric that measures it.">

<Stage label="Clicks" value="CTR" width="80" />
<Stage label="Inquiries" value="CPL" width="60" />
<Stage label="Booked consults" value="Cost per consult" width="42" />
<Stage label="Attended consults" value="Show rate" width="28" />
</Funnel>

- **CTR (click-through rate):** the share of people who see an ad and click it.
- **Conversion rate:** the share of visitors who submit a form or request a consult.
- **CPL (cost per lead):** ad spend divided by inquiries.
- **CPA (cost per acquisition):** spend divided by the result you care about, which for a med spa should be a booked or attended consult, not a lead.
- **ROAS (return on ad spend):** revenue from ads divided by ad spend, which understates good campaigns if it only counts the first visit.

We track conversions on what we build: forms, modals, and landing pages.

When it's needed to follow an inquiry through to a booking, we connect your booking software for conversion tracking.

The wider measurement picture is in [attribution and call tracking](/blog/medspa-attribution-call-tracking/).

<Callout type="note">

Tracking pixels on a medical spa website can send data tying a person to a treatment to ad platforms, which can be an impermissible disclosure of protected health information.

[HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) explains the risk, and your compliance advisor should review your setup.

</Callout>

## <span className="he">✅</span> Questions to ask before you sign

Whoever you hire, these questions make marketing quotes comparable.

<Checklist>

- Does the quote include ad spend, or only the management fee?
- Is the fee flat, or a percentage of what you spend on ads?
- Is ad spend paid directly to the platform, or marked up?
- What's the minimum term, and why that length?
- Does the report show booked consults, or only clicks and leads?
- Do you own the landing pages and content if you leave?

</Checklist>
