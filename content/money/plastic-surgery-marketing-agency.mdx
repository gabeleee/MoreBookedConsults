## <span className="he">🖥️</span> A plastic surgery website that converts, without a redesign

Almost every plastic surgery marketing company leads with web design, and they're right that the website is where consults are won or lost.

Where we differ is the fix: instead of a full website build that resets everything, we build the pages that sell your procedures and test the parts of your site that leak.

## <span className="he">📝</span> Plastic surgery SEO: procedure pages and topical authority

Patients search by procedure: "rhinoplasty near me," "tummy tuck cost," "facelift recovery."

So plastic surgery SEO is mostly procedure-page SEO, built on a topical map that covers every procedure, concern, and question your patients search.

Each procedure gets a thorough page, internally linked into a structure that tells Google your practice is the local answer for that procedure.

That patient-education content is how topical authority works: the practice that answers the most questions, best, becomes the source Google trusts.

## <span className="he">📍</span> Local SEO and Google Business Profile

For "plastic surgeon near me," Google shows a map and three listings before any website appears.

Winning that local pack starts with [Google Business Profile optimization](/blog/plastic-surgery-google-business-profile/): the right primary category, your procedures, photos, and accurate hours.

Multi-surgeon practices have an extra decision: how listings map to each surgeon versus the practice, so the signals reinforce each other instead of splitting.

Then comes citation cleanup, so your name, address, and phone number match across every directory Google checks.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> HIPAA and tracking pixels

Any plastic surgery digital marketing agency that installs tracking should know this risk: pixels and analytics can send data tying a person to a procedure, or to being a patient, to third-party ad platforms.

That can be an impermissible disclosure of protected health information under HIPAA.

We set tracking up with that risk in mind and collect less when in doubt, but we don't claim a certified HIPAA-compliant setup, and your compliance advisor should review yours.

| Services | Landing pages, CRO, local SEO, Meta ads | SEO, PPC, social, PR, web, reputation | A roadmap and oversight |
