More Booked Consults and Plastix Marketing both work with aesthetic practices, and both do SEO and Meta ads.

They're built for different jobs.

Plastix Marketing runs your whole marketing operation: every channel, your website, and a CRM it sets up and connects.

More Booked Consults works on three things only, for a practice that already has its website, CRM and booking software in place.

So the choice comes down to how much you need done, and by how many people.

## <span className="he">🧰</span> What do More Booked Consults and Plastix Marketing each offer?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/plastix.webp" small="/images/agencies/plastix-800.webp" alt="Plastix Marketing medical spa marketing page" width="1280" height="700" caption="plastixmarketing.com, September 2026" />

[Plastix Marketing](https://plastixmarketing.com/medical-spa-marketing/) describes itself as the growth agency for plastic surgery practices and medical spas, and also lists dermatology, cosmetic dentistry and orthodontics.

- **Services:** SEO, AI search optimization, content marketing, social media, search and digital advertising, lead generation, sales enablement, chatbots, web design, branding, video marketing, marketing automation, CRM support, consulting and hosting.
- **Ads:** Google Search ads, Facebook and Instagram ads, native ads, display and geofencing.
- **CRM:** a certified partner with HubSpot, Keap and SharpSpring that supports CRM setup, implementation and maintenance in every growth plan, and says it can connect the CRM to your website, its reporting and your electronic health records platform.
- **Team:** its about page lists 15 specialists across every channel, including SEO, paid ads, creative, web and account directors (self-reported).
- **Who it says it isn't for:** practices looking for the cheapest marketing option, wanting Groupon-style promotions, or expecting ROI within the first 30 days.

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

More Booked Consults is a one-person consultancy for med spas, plastic surgeons, injectors, laser clinics and cosmetic dermatology practices.

- **[Conversion rate optimization](/medspa-cro/):** one controlled test a month on the page or form that loses the most booked consults.
- **[Local SEO](/medspa-seo/):** treatment and city pages, Google Business Profile work and citations.
- **[Managed Meta ads](/medspa-advertising/):** 8–12 new static image ads a month, landing pages built for them, and one landing-page test a month.
- **First 30 days:** a multi-step consult form built and live on your site, and local SEO work started.
- **Not offered:** Google Ads, website rebuilds, CRM setup or video ads.

Gabe Meierotto does the work himself, so there's no account manager between you and the person running your tests, pages and ads.

That also means there's no bench: Plastix Marketing has separate specialists for SEO, paid ads, creative and web, and More Booked Consults has one person.

## <span className="he">💵</span> What do More Booked Consults and Plastix Marketing cost?

Only one of the two publishes a price.

More Booked Consults lists every price on its [pricing page](/pricing/).

| Service | Price | Minimum term |
|---|---|---|
| CRO | $2,500 a month | 6 months |
| Local SEO | $2,500 a month | 6 months |
| CRO + local SEO | $5,000 a month | 6 months |
| Managed Meta ads | $3,000 a month, plus $2,500 one-time setup | 3 months |

For Meta ads, you pay your ad spend directly to Meta (minimum $3,000 a month), with no markup and no percentage of spend.

Plastix Marketing has no pricing page.

Its [Growth Plan page](https://plastixmarketing.com/growth-plan/) asks you to book a 20-minute call, and its form asks your monthly marketing budget in four ranges, from under $5,000 to $20,000+ a month.

That's a qualifying question, not a price list.

Its terms say fees are set in each order: a monthly retainer, any paid-ads management fee, setup fees and add-ons.

The two quotes won't cover the same work, so compare scope before you compare totals.

## <span className="he">🔒</span> Who owns your accounts, website and data?

With More Booked Consults, the practice owns its accounts from the first day.

The Meta ad account, pixel and audiences sit in your own Business Manager, and More Booked Consults works inside it as a partner.

Your website, domain, Google Business Profile and analytics stay in your name.

Landing pages, ads, test results and reports built during the engagement are yours to keep when it ends.

Plastix Marketing's terms say ad account ownership is set in your order, and that if the order is silent, you own your ad accounts.

They also say you own your pixels, conversion data and audiences built on property you own.

Where it runs campaigns in an account of its own, its terms say it isn't obligated to transfer that account's shell or its proprietary structures.

Custom work made for you transfers to you on full payment, while its own templates, frameworks and tools stay with Plastix Marketing under a license to you.

If Plastix Marketing hosts your site, its terms give you 48 hours after cancelling a hosting plan to move it.

Because the CRM it sets up is HubSpot, Keap or SharpSpring, your contact records sit in a mainstream tool rather than in an agency's own software.

## <span className="he">🛡️</span> Does either company sign a BAA?

Plastix Marketing does, in its published terms.

They say it acts as a Business Associate when its work needs access to a practice system containing patient health information, and that a BAA is signed before that access is given.

More Booked Consults doesn't publish a Business Associate Agreement, so ask before you share any patient data.

A BAA covers how a vendor handles patient data, not whether your tracking pixels send health data to ad platforms.

Read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before anyone installs tracking on your site.

## <span className="he">📈</span> What results does each company publish?

This is the widest gap between the two, and it favors Plastix Marketing.

Plastix Marketing publishes a [client case study](https://plastixmarketing.com/portfolio/buckhead-plastic-surgery-case-study/) for Buckhead Plastic Surgery and LUX Med Spa: a 164% lead increase and 3,500+ new leads in 18 months.

It also says its average client stays 3.2 years.

Those numbers are self-reported and we haven't audited them.

More Booked Consults was founded in 2026 and has no published client case studies yet.

Its published track record belongs to Gabe Meierotto personally, from his in-house role as Director of CRO at LaserAway from 2018 to 2023.

In that role his testing program took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% across more than 2,600 tested variations (documented in [Intellimize's published case study](/laseraway-intellimize-case-study.pdf); see [results](/results/)).

Those are his results at one large brand, not agency client results, and More Booked Consults doesn't promise or guarantee results.

If a record of client outcomes at practices like yours matters most to you, Plastix Marketing has one to show and More Booked Consults doesn't yet.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Should you pick More Booked Consults or Plastix Marketing?

**Pick Plastix Marketing if:**

- You want one team to run SEO, content, social, video, Google and Meta ads as one connected plan.
- You need a new website, branding, or a CRM set up and wired to your site and health records.
- You want Google Ads managed, which More Booked Consults doesn't do.
- You want a BAA in writing, published client case studies, and a team with more than one person on your account.
- You can commit to 6 or 12 months and aren't shopping on price, which Plastix Marketing itself says isn't its fit.

**Pick More Booked Consults if:**

- You already have a website, CRM and booking software you're keeping.
- Your gap is specific: visitors who don't book, weak map-pack rankings, or Meta ads that need landing pages built for them.
- You want to see the price and terms before any call.
- You'd rather work directly with one senior person than with an account team.
- You're comfortable hiring a company founded in 2026 on its founder's in-house record.

Neither is the cheaper or better version of the other; they sell different amounts of work.

## <span className="he">✅</span> What should you ask either company before you sign?

Get these answered in writing, whichever one you pick.

<Checklist>

- What is the total monthly fee, setup fee and ad-spend arrangement?
- What is the minimum term, the notice period and the cost of leaving early?
- Are the ad accounts, domain and Google Business Profile in my name today?
- Which landing pages, website files and automations do I keep if I leave?
- Will you sign a BAA before touching patient data, and how does your tracking avoid sending it to ad platforms?
- Who exactly does the work on my account, and how do I reach them?
- Will the monthly report show booked consults, not just leads and clicks?
