Influx Marketing builds premium brands for cash-pay aesthetic practices: custom websites, original photo and video, and the SEO and ads that send people to them.

Owners who search for Influx Marketing alternatives usually fall into one of two groups.

Some want the same design-led, full-service model from a different team, a different size, or on terms they can see up front.

Others already have a beautiful site that doesn't book enough consults, and want a conversion specialist instead of another redesign.

This page covers both groups: five agencies, and a plain "pick this one if" for each.

## <span className="he">✅</span> Influx Marketing review: what does it do well?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/influx.webp" small="/images/agencies/influx-800.webp" alt="Influx Marketing med spa marketing page" width="1280" height="700" caption="influxmarketing.com, September 2026" />

[Influx Marketing](https://www.influxmarketing.com/medspa-marketing/) lists offices in Pasadena, Salt Lake City and Tampa, and serves med spas, plastic surgery, cosmetic dentistry and cash-pay healthcare.

This isn't a rating; it's a fair summary of Influx Marketing's strengths, taken from its own site.

- **Custom design:** its web design page says every site gets a custom design system, and that professional web design should feel unique to each practice.
- **Original photo and video:** its production team handles concept, production and post-production, and its site argues original imagery can't be replaced by stock or AI-generated images.
- **Full service around the site:** SEO, Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, content and copywriting, social media management, email and analytics.

Influx Marketing is also building Growthstack, an all-in-one growth platform for med spas, which was on a waitlist with demos by request in September 2026.

## <span className="he">🏆</span> The 5 best Influx Marketing alternatives

### 1. More Booked Consults: best when the site looks good but doesn't book

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

**Best for:** med spas and aesthetic practices that already have a good-looking site and steady traffic, but not enough booked consults.

More Booked Consults is a med spa and plastic surgery marketing agency focused on conversion (landing pages, CRO), local SEO and managed Meta ads.

It's run by Gabe Meierotto, LaserAway's in-house Director of CRO from 2018 to 2023, and works only with aesthetic practices.

- **Services:** conversion-focused landing pages, [conversion rate optimization](/medspa-cro/) including new components like multi-step forms and exit-intent modals, [local SEO](/medspa-seo/) built on topical maps with Google Business Profile work and citation cleanup, and [managed Meta ads](/medspa-advertising/) with fresh static ad creative every month.
- **Pricing (published):** CRO $2,500/month, local SEO $2,500/month, both for $5,000/month; managed Meta ads $3,000/month plus a $2,500 one-time setup, with ad spend paid by you directly to Meta and never marked up.
- **Contract:** 6-month minimum on CRO and on local SEO, 3-month minimum on managed ads.
- **HIPAA:** no BAA is published; we give guidance on how [tracking pixels can leak patient data](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/).
- **Not offered:** full website builds or redesigns, Google Ads, organic social, email and SMS, CRM setup, review campaigns, photo or video.

At LaserAway, Gabe's testing program took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% across 2,600+ tested variations (documented in [Intellimize's published case study](/laseraway-intellimize-case-study.pdf); see [results](/results/)); those are his results in a former in-house role, not agency client results.

**Choose it over Influx Marketing if** your brand and site already look the part, and what you need is more of your visitors turning into booked consults, better map-pack visibility, or Meta ads sent to pages built to convert.

**Pick someone else if** you need a new brand, a new website, or photo and video production; More Booked Consults doesn't do those.

## <span className="he">📝</span> Questions to ask any agency before you switch

Get these answered in writing before you sign anything new, whichever agency you pick.

<Checklist>

- What are the exit terms: minimum term, renewal, notice period, and what's owed if we end early?
- Who owns the ad accounts, the website, the domain and the Google Business Profile, and are they in my name today?
- Will you sign a BAA, and how does your tracking avoid sending patient health data to ad platforms?
- Will your monthly report show booked consults by channel, not just traffic, leads and clicks?
- What happens to my landing pages, forms and photography if I leave: do I keep them, and on what platform?
- Do you mark up ad spend or charge a percentage of it?

</Checklist>

Ask your current agency the same ownership questions before you give notice.

Tracking pixels on lead forms can leak patient information, so read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before any new agency installs tracking on your site.
