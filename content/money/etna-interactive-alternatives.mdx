## <span className="he">✅</span> Etna Interactive review: what does it do well?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/etna-ps.webp" small="/images/agencies/etna-ps-800.webp" alt="Etna Interactive plastic surgery marketing page" width="1280" height="700" caption="etnainteractive.com, September 2026" />

[Etna Interactive](https://www.etnainteractive.com/clients/plastic-surgery-marketing/) has worked in elective healthcare marketing since 2002, runs fully remote with a mailing address in San Luis Obispo, California, and serves plastic surgery, facial plastic surgery, med spa, dermatology and ophthalmology practices.

A fair summary of its strengths, from its own site:

- **Full service with its own tools:** websites, SEO, answer engine optimization, search and social advertising, social media, email, content, reputation management and local optimization, plus six in-house products including Core lead management and Curator B&A before-and-after galleries.
- **Lead tracking built in:** its Core page says Core is a standard feature in every Etna marketing plan, records the referral source of each lead, and is HIPAA-compliant.
- **Staff training:** a training and staff development service that benchmarks lead-to-consultation and consultation-to-service rates against other practices.
- **A structured partnership:** a free assessment, a proposal within a week, monthly reporting, semi-annual strategy meetings and yearly goal setting; its site says you own all your assets if you part ways.
- **Proof (self-reported):** Karter Advanced Facial Plastic Surgery reached 5.2x return from paid search after 12 months, and Camp Plastic Surgery's revenue more than tripled with website traffic up 735% since 2016.

Etna's about page is also candid about fit: it says it may not be the right partner if low price is your main criterion or you see online marketing as a short-term pursuit.

### 1. More Booked Consults: best for turning inquiries into booked consults

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

**Best for:** surgeons and aesthetic practices that already get traffic and inquiries but don't book enough consults.

More Booked Consults is a med spa and plastic surgery marketing agency focused on conversion (landing pages, CRO), local SEO and managed Meta ads, run by Gabe Meierotto, LaserAway's in-house Director of CRO from 2018 to 2023.

- **Services:** conversion-focused landing pages, [consult-funnel CRO](/plastic-surgery-cro/) including new components like multi-step forms and exit-intent modals, [local SEO](/plastic-surgery-seo/) built on topical maps with Google Business Profile work and citation cleanup, and [managed Meta ads](/plastic-surgeon-advertising/) with fresh static ad creative every month.
- **Pricing (published):** CRO $2,500/month, local SEO $2,500/month, both for $5,000/month; managed Meta ads $3,000/month plus a $2,500 one-time setup, with ad spend paid by you directly to Meta and never marked up.
- **Contract:** 6-month minimum on CRO and on local SEO, 3-month minimum on managed ads.
- **HIPAA:** no BAA is published; we give guidance on how [tracking pixels can leak patient data](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/).
- **Not offered:** Google Ads, website rebuilds, organic social, email and SMS, CRM setup, review campaigns, photo or video, staff training.

At LaserAway, Gabe's testing program took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% (see [results](/results/)); those are his results in a former in-house role, not agency client results.

**Choose it over Etna Interactive if** your gap is conversion, local visibility or Meta ads, and you want published prices and a senior operator doing the work.

**Pick someone else if** you want one firm for everything, including Google Ads, a new website, galleries, social posting and coordinator training.

**Best for:** established surgical practices that want Etna's kind of full-service scope from another long-tenured, surgery-first firm.

[Plastic Surgery Studios](https://www.plasticsurgerystudios.com/) was established in 1999 in Rancho Cucamonga, California, and says it works almost exclusively with plastic surgeons, cosmetic surgeons, dermatologists and cosmetic dentists.

- **Services:** web design, content, SEO, PPC, social media, branding, photography and video, loyalty programs, and StudiosCRM, its CRM and ROI dashboard for cosmetic practices.
- **Pricing:** not published; its terms require a non-refundable deposit before work begins.
- **Contract (published):** services paid in monthly installments can't be cancelled in the first 12 months; after that, either side can end services with 30 days' written notice.
- **Ownership (published):** you keep the final website, while it keeps working files, source code and third-party licensed software.
- **HIPAA:** its terms place HIPAA compliance on the client; no BAA is published.

**Choose it over Etna Interactive if** you want a similar surgery-first, full-service firm and prefer contract terms you can read before the first call.

## <span className="he">📋</span> Questions to ask any agency before you switch

<Checklist>

- What is the minimum term, the notice period, and any early-termination fee?
- Who owns the ad accounts, the website, the domain and the Google Business Profile, and are they in my name today?
- Will you sign a BAA, and how does your tracking avoid sending health data to ad platforms?
- Does your monthly report show booked consults and surgeries by channel, not just leads and clicks?
- What happens to the landing pages, galleries, lead history and call-tracking numbers if I leave?
- Do you mark up ad spend or charge a percentage of it?

</Checklist>

Ask the outgoing agency the same ownership questions before you give notice.

Start with [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) if you're unsure what your current setup sends to Meta or Google, and read the [lead management guide](/blog/plastic-surgery-lead-management/) before you move your lead history to a new system.
