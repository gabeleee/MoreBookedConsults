More Booked Consults and Influx Marketing both work with med spas and plastic surgery practices, and both do SEO and Meta ads.

They are built for different problems.

Influx Marketing builds the whole presence: the brand, a custom website, original photo and video, and the marketing channels around them.

More Booked Consults does not build websites at all; it works on the site and traffic you already have, so more visitors book a consult.

That difference in scope drives the rest: team size, what's published, and what you'd be paying for.

## <span className="he">🧰</span> What do More Booked Consults and Influx Marketing each offer?

<Photo src="/images/agencies/influx.webp" small="/images/agencies/influx-800.webp" alt="Influx Marketing med spa marketing page" width="1280" height="700" caption="influxmarketing.com, September 2026" />

[Influx Marketing](https://www.influxmarketing.com/medspa-marketing/) lists offices in Pasadena, Salt Lake City and Tampa, and serves med spas, plastic surgery, cosmetic dentistry and cash-pay healthcare practices.

- **Website design and development:** its [web design page](https://www.influxmarketing.com/services/web-design-development/) says strategy, UX design, content, media, development, technical SEO and project management all stay in-house.
- **Photo and video production:** from concept through production and post-production, and its site says original images can't be substituted with stock or AI-generated imagery.
- **Advertising:** its [advertising page](https://www.influxmarketing.com/services/digital-advertising/) covers Google search ads and Facebook and Instagram ads, and shows Google Partner and Facebook Partner badges.
- **Everything around it:** SEO, content strategy and copywriting, social media management, email marketing, and analytics with a real-time dashboard and lead tracking.
- **Growthstack:** a platform Influx Marketing calls an all-in-one growth platform for med spas, which was on a waitlist with demos by request in October 2026.

<Photo src="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults.webp" small="/images/agencies/more-booked-consults-800.webp" alt="More Booked Consults homepage" width="1280" height="700" caption="morebookedconsults.com, September 2026" />

More Booked Consults is a one-person consultancy that works only with aesthetic practices: med spas, plastic surgeons, injectors, laser clinics and cosmetic dermatology.

It's run by Gabe Meierotto, former Director of CRO at LaserAway (2018–2023), who does the work himself.

- **[Conversion rate optimization](/medspa-cro/):** one controlled test a month on the page or form that loses the most booked consults.
- **[Local SEO](/medspa-seo/):** treatment and city pages, Google Business Profile work and citations.
- **[Managed Meta ads](/medspa-advertising/):** 8–12 new static image ads a month, landing pages built for them, and one landing-page test a month.
- **Not offered:** Google Ads, website rebuilds, CRM setup or video ads.

Those three services are the whole list, so social media management, email marketing and photo or video production come from Influx Marketing, not from More Booked Consults.

With More Booked Consults' managed Meta ads, you pay your ad spend directly to Meta, with a $3,000 a month minimum.

The spend is never marked up, and the fee is never a percentage of it.

As of October 2026, we found no prices, retainers, management fees or minimum ad spend on Influx Marketing's home, med spa, web design or advertising pages.

Its pages direct you to contact the team for a quote.

A retainer that includes a custom site and original photo and video covers far more than a conversion or SEO retainer, so compare scope before you compare price.

Ask Influx Marketing for the monthly fee, any design or production fees, and how ad spend is paid, in writing.

## <span className="he">🔒</span> Who owns your website, ad accounts and data?

Both companies publish ownership language, and both put the core assets with the practice.

Influx Marketing's web design page says the practice owns the domain, brand assets, custom content, commissioned photography, design files, client data and project-specific code, as defined in the agreement.

It adds that third-party tools and web platforms keep their own license terms, and that Influx Marketing documents those terms and provides the access the client needs.

Its advertising page doesn't say who owns the Google and Meta ad accounts, so ask.

With More Booked Consults, the Meta ad account, pixel and audiences sit in the practice's own Business Manager, and More Booked Consults works inside it as a partner.

The website, domain, Google Business Profile and analytics stay in the practice's name.

Landing pages, ads, test results and reports built during the engagement are the practice's to keep when it ends.

Neither company publishes a Business Associate Agreement: we found no HIPAA or BAA statement on Influx Marketing's site, and More Booked Consults doesn't publish one either.

A BAA also doesn't settle whether tracking pixels send patient data to ad platforms, so read [HIPAA and website tracking](/blog/hipaa-website-tracking/) before either company installs tracking.

- **Influx Marketing's client averages (self-reported):** a 3X increase in website speed, 500+ more keyword rankings, 200% more organic website traffic and a 250% increase in new patient leads.

What More Booked Consults can point to is Gabe Meierotto's own record as LaserAway's in-house Director of CRO from 2018 to 2023.

His testing program there took sitewide conversion from 3% to 11% across 2,600+ tested variations, documented in [Intellimize's published case study](/laseraway-intellimize-case-study.pdf) and sourced on the [results page](/results/).

Those are his results in a former in-house role, not client results of More Booked Consults.

More Booked Consults doesn't promise or guarantee results.

## <span className="he">🧭</span> Should you pick Influx Marketing or More Booked Consults?

**Pick Influx Marketing if:**

- You need a new website or a rebrand, which More Booked Consults doesn't do.
- You want original photography and video produced for your practice.
- You want Google search ads, social media management or email marketing handled alongside SEO and Meta ads.
- You want a full team with a client portfolio and testimonials you can check, not a company founded in 2026.
- You're fine getting pricing and contract terms on a call.

**Pick More Booked Consults if:**

- Your site already looks good and gets traffic, but not enough of it books.
- You want to rank in Google's map pack for your treatments.
- You want Meta ads sent to landing pages built and tested for them.
- You want prices, minimum terms and cancellation rules published before you talk to anyone.
- You'd rather work directly with one senior person than with an account team.

Some practices need both jobs in order: a new site first, then conversion work once it has traffic.

## <span className="he">✅</span> What should you ask either company before you sign?

Get these answered in writing, whichever one you pick.

<Checklist>

- What is the total monthly fee, any setup, design or production fee, and how is ad spend paid?
- What is the minimum term, the renewal rule, the notice period and the cost of ending early?
- Are the ad accounts, domain, website and Google Business Profile in my name today?
- What do I keep if I leave: pages, photography, ad creative, reports, and on what platform?
- Will you sign a BAA before touching patient data, and how does your tracking avoid sending it to ad platforms?
- Who will actually do the work on my account, and how often will I hear from them?
- Will the monthly report show booked consults by channel, not just traffic and clicks?

</Checklist>
