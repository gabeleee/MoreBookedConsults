Laser clinics live and die on local search.

Patients don't search for "a laser clinic."

They search for "laser hair removal near me," "tattoo removal" plus their city, or "IPL photofacial" plus their neighborhood, and Google answers with a map and three listings before any website appears.

That's where laser clinic SEO starts: getting your clinic into that pack, for each treatment you offer, before the busy season arrives.

## <span className="he">🗺️</span> Why the map pack matters most

For a treatment search with local intent, the map pack usually sits above the regular results, especially on phones.

The three clinics in it get seen first, compared first, and called first.

Everything else on the page is competing for what's left.

So the first job of laser clinic SEO isn't blog content or backlinks.

It's making your Google Business Profile the most relevant, most trusted answer within a few miles of your front door.

## <span className="he">🧱</span> The four things that rank a laser clinic

<Cards>

<Card icon="pin" title="Google Business Profile">

The right primary category (Google has specific ones, such as laser hair removal service and tattoo removal service), every treatment listed as a service, real photos, and accurate hours.

</Card>

<Card icon="star" title="Reviews">

Count, recency, and a steady pace matter more than a one-time push. Replies should never confirm that a reviewer is a patient.

</Card>

<Card icon="page" title="A page per treatment">

Laser hair removal, tattoo removal, IPL, and resurfacing each get a page that answers the questions patients actually ask, including price and number of sessions.

</Card>

<Card icon="link" title="Citations and local links">

Your clinic's name, address, and phone number matching everywhere they appear, plus links from local sources Google trusts.

</Card>

</Cards>

None of these is a trick.

They're the signals Google has said it uses, done thoroughly and kept up every month, which is exactly what most clinics don't have time for.

## <span className="he">🎯</span> Rank for treatments, not just "laser clinic"

Very few patients search for your business category.

They search for the treatment they want, and each treatment is its own ranking battle.

A clinic can rank well for laser hair removal and be invisible for tattoo removal in the same city, because Google judges them as different questions.

That's why the content plan is built treatment by treatment:

- **One page per treatment** you want to be found for, with the city or neighborhood you serve
- **Price and session answers,** because "how much is laser hair removal" is one of the most common questions patients ask before booking
- **Body areas and skin types** where they change the answer, so the page fits the patient's actual situation
- **Before-and-after galleries and FAQs** that give Google, and the patient, a reason to trust the page

If you run more than one location, each clinic needs its own profile and its own location page, and [multi-location SEO](/blog/medspa-multi-location-seo/) covers how to keep them from competing with each other.

## <span className="he">☀️</span> Timing: rank before the season, not during it

Laser hair removal in particular is seasonal.

Many patients start a series in fall or winter so they're finished by summer, and demand builds through the spring.

SEO takes months to mature, so the work has to start well before demand does.

Most markets have at least one national laser chain with a large marketing budget.

That sounds like a losing fight, but the map pack doesn't work like paid ads.

## <span className="he">✅</span> Laser clinic SEO checklist

Whether you hire someone or do it yourself, these are the basics most clinics are missing.

<Checklist>

- The primary Google Business Profile category matches your main treatment
- Every treatment you offer is listed as a service on the profile
- Name, address, and phone number match on your site and every directory
- A routine asks every happy patient for a review, every week
- Every review gets a reply that never confirms the reviewer is a patient
- Each treatment you want to rank for has its own page on your site
- Those pages answer price, number of sessions, and who the treatment is right for

</Checklist>
