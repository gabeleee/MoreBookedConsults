Skin tightening competes less with other med spa treatments and more with the idea of surgery, and that framing is its biggest marketing opportunity.

A large audience wants firmer skin but isn't ready for a facelift, and skin tightening is the answer that meets them there.

## <span className="he">⚖️</span> Ad-policy constraints

Skin tightening avoids the prescription-drug rules that complicate Botox ads, but it isn't free of restrictions.

Meta's [Health and Wellness ad standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/) still treat it as a cosmetic procedure.

- **Target 18 and older** for any cosmetic procedure ad.
- **No statements of inferiority,** so copy can't attack the viewer's skin, jawline, or body.
- **No timeframe promises without qualifiers,** such as a guaranteed result by a set date.
- **Before-and-after images are allowed** under the current version, with written patient consent, but Meta has revised this rule more than once.

Meta can also restrict the data your pixel sends back if it classifies your site as health-related, which the [Meta health and wellness ad policy guide](/blog/medspa-healthcare-ad-policy/) explains.

In our experience Google clicks for "skin tightening near me" searches are very expensive, and Meta ads get med spas better results, so Meta is what we run, as our [med spa advertising](/medspa-advertising/) page explains.

Keep lifting and tightening claims honest and non-guaranteed in every channel, since results are gradual and subtle.
