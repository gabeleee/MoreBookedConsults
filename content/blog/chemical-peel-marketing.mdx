Chemical peels are a workhorse skin treatment that too many med spas sell one at a time, leaving both results and revenue on the table.

The better approach ties them to a season, a skin goal, and a recurring plan.

## <span className="he">📈</span> Demand and seasonality

<Callout type="key">

Peels increase sun sensitivity, so fall and winter are the natural selling seasons, when patients can more easily avoid heavy sun afterward.

Marketing peels as the cooler-months skin reset fits the treatment and fills demand in a season when body-contouring interest quiets.

</Callout>

That counter-seasonal fit makes peels a smart thing to promote exactly when your summer-driven treatments slow down.

## <span className="he">💰</span> Offer economics: series and membership

Most skin concerns, texture, tone, mild scarring, improve over a course of peels rather than a single treatment.

<Compare>

<Side title="Sold one at a time" tone="no">

- A single peel for a concern that needs a course
- A modest per-peel price, one visit at a time
- Results and revenue left on the table

</Side>

<Side title="Sold as a series" tone="yes">

- Tied to a specific skin goal
- Or folded into a [membership](/blog/medspa-membership-programs/) alongside facials
- Fits the clinical reality and turns a modest per-peel price into meaningful recurring revenue

</Side>

</Compare>

The [intro-to-series](/blog/medspa-intro-offers/) framing works well: a first peel that leads into a plan.

## <span className="he">⚖️</span> Ad-policy constraints

Chemical peels are topical treatments that avoid the prescription-drug rules that complicate Botox ads, but they aren't free of restrictions.

Meta's [Health and Wellness ad standards](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/) still treat them as a cosmetic procedure.

- **Target 18 and older** for any cosmetic procedure ad.
- **No statements of inferiority,** so copy can't attack the viewer's skin or complexion.
- **No timeframe promises without qualifiers,** such as a guaranteed result by a set date.
- **Before-and-after images are allowed** under the current version, with written patient consent, but Meta has revised this rule more than once.

Meta can also restrict the data your pixel sends back if it classifies your site as health-related, which the [Meta health and wellness ad policy guide](/blog/medspa-healthcare-ad-policy/) explains.

In our experience Google clicks for "chemical peel near me" searches are very expensive, and Meta ads get med spas better results, so Meta is what we run, as our [med spa advertising](/medspa-advertising/) page explains.

Keep results and downtime claims honest in every channel.

## <span className="he">🎯</span> The conversion angle

Peel patients are chasing a specific skin outcome, so lead with the goal, not the acid.
